          Whose word is it
          
      
          DNO's words describe what it saw from outside. Demos uses its own words for a node's health and a validator's status. DNO counts validator statuses as the public seeds list them; it does not see a node's own health.
          
      
          
        
          
          
          DNO's words · seen from outside
          
          
          
            
          stable
          this cycle's reading of the public seeds. Not ok, not ACTIVE.
          
            
          strong
          every height counted sits within ±25 blocks of the median, and the spread is at most 20 blocks.
          
            
          sufficient
          at least two seeds reported their own height, and the observation is at most 300 s old. It describes DNO's vantage, not the network.
          
            
          peer-reported
          what a public peerlist said. Never confirmed by DNO.
          
            
          at the seeds' height
          a validator's own height, in its answer to DNO, within ±25 blocks of the seeds' median. Not Demos' sync check.
          
            
          first counted
          on DNO's own clock. A peer-listed identity: when a public peerlist first listed it, once a second has. An ACTIVE key: the first agreed validators list that showed it. Not the chain's firstSeen.
          
          
          
        
          
        
          
          
          Demos' words · not DNO's
          
          
          
            
          ok degraded dormant failing
          a node's own GET /health. DNO does not read it.
          
            
          ACTIVE UNSTAKING EXITED
          a row in the on-chain validators table. DNO counts ACTIVE and UNSTAKING rows as two public seeds list them; EXITED rows are not in that list. It decides none of them.
          
            
          out of sync
          consensus Phase 1: a validator skipped from shard composition for those blocks. Not slashing. DNO does not see it.
